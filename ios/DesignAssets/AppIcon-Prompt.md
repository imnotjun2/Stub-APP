# Stub App Icon prompt

Tool mode: built-in `image_gen`.

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: square iOS App Icon for the app Stub / 生活存根

Primary request: Create a polished, immediately recognizable app icon inspired by the attached cream-paper Stub interface. Show one minimal torn paper ticket stub as the central symbol, with a subtle perforated edge or tear line and one restrained brick-red stamped accent. No letters, no words, no numerals, no airline or cinema logos.

Style/medium: tactile editorial paper collage, refined independent iOS app, not skeuomorphic clutter.

Composition/framing: square 1:1, centered symbol, generous safe padding, strong silhouette that remains readable at 60px, artwork fills the full square with no transparent margin.

Lighting/mood: soft warm daylight, quiet nostalgic mood, very subtle paper grain and shallow shadow.

Color palette: warm cream `#F6F1E7`, ink brown `#282421`, brick red `#B94738`, optional tiny old-gold `#8B5F12`.

Materials/textures: convincing cotton paper fibers, softly torn edge, restrained emboss or stamp texture.

Constraints: no text, no watermark, no mockup device, no rounded-corner mask baked into the artwork, no gradients unrelated to paper lighting, no busy background, no extra objects.
