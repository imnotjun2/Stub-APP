# Prototype asset attributions

## Airline identification marks

The bundled `MU`, `CA`, and `CZ` raster marks were extracted from `airlogos@1.0.7`, distributed under the MIT license: <https://www.npmjs.com/package/airlogos>.

They are included only to identify China Eastern Airlines, Air China, and China Southern Airlines inside this private product prototype. Airline names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. A production release must replace this demo asset set with a licensed airline-brand provider and its required attribution or usage terms.
